Stack Over  ÞToo many 'STR' numbers.Example:  0000    STR   X0001  ; STR  0001    STR   C0001  ; STR  0002    OUT   Y0001  ; (two 'STR' numbers)   .       .      .   .       .      .Since one 'OUT' instruction only needs one 'STR' instruction, the "Stack Over" error occurs.Back Screen Shot

---------- topico 16  TOPICPOS=84596  
Stack Under  Þ'STR' number is not enough.Example:  0000    STR   X0001  ; STR Number =1  0001    CNT   V0001           0002          00010   .       .      .   .       .      .Since one 'CNT' instruction needs two 'STR' instructions, the "Stack Under" error occurs.Back Screen Shot

The "F08 ENDS Error" error occurs because there is no function 'F-08' in the program.Back Screen Shot

FUN.Double Used  ÞIf this error occurs, please check each of the functions, 'F52', 'F53', 'F55', 'F58' and 'F59', if they are double used in the program.Example:  0000    F-52   V0001          0001           Y0001          0002           X0001  0003    F-52   V0001          0004           Y0001          0005           X0001There are two 'F-52' functions in the program, so the "FUN.Double Used" error occurs.Back Screen Shot
